What's covered

Protection for every part
of your business.

Core cover for the risks founders actually face — not generic commercial packages.

Professional indemnity
Covers claims of negligence or errors in your services or software — critical for B2B SaaS and consulting.
Cyber protection
Data breach response, ransomware, and third-party liability. Covers ICO fines and GDPR notification costs.
Employers' liability
Legal requirement for UK businesses with employees. Protects your business against employee injury or illness claims.
Directors & officers
Protects founders and board members from personal liability arising from company decisions — required by many investors.
Business interruption
Covers lost revenue when an insured event forces your business to halt or significantly slow down operations.
Product liability
Essential for hardware, marketplace, and consumer product startups. Covers claims related to products causing harm.
IP & legal defence
Covers legal costs from IP disputes, contract disagreements, and regulatory actions — increasingly common as you scale.
Key person cover
Protects your business financially if a critical founder or employee becomes unable to work due to illness or injury.
